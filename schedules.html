Application Requirements

Complete documentation needed for qualification assessment

  • 📋
    Valid Government ID

    Philippine passport, driver's license, or National ID

  • 🎓
    Educational Background Certificate

    High school diploma or equivalent (original and copy)

  • 🏢
    Work Experience Letter

    Proof of relevant industry experience (if applicable)

  • 📸
    Recent Passport Photos

    4x6 inches, white background (2 pieces)

  • ✍️
    Assessment Registration Form

    Completed and signed form available at registration desk

  • 💳
    Assessment Fee Receipt

    Proof of payment (varies by qualification)
